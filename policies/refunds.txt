1. No single refund may exceed $200.
2. Cumulative refunds in any 24-hour period may not exceed $1,000 per agent.
3. Cumulative refunds in any 30-day period may not exceed $10,000 per customer.
4. Refunds are only permitted for orders placed within the past 90 days.
5. Any refund over $50 requires a reason code from the approved list ("defective", "not_as_described", "shipping_damaged", "duplicate_charge", "customer_dissatisfaction").
6. No refunds may be issued to customers with account status equal to "fraud_flag" or "chargeback_pending".
7. Refunds over $100 trigger a human-in-the-loop approval requirement.
