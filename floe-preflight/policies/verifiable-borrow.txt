Verifiable Agent Borrow Policy

This policy governs whether a Floe instant_borrow request from an AI agent should be cleared. Every clause must be satisfied for the borrow to proceed.

1. The requested borrowAmount must be less than or equal to the agent's remaining on-chain credit line, computed as borrowLimit minus borrowed in the OperatorPermission for the operator address.

2. The requested maxInterestRateBps must be less than or equal to the maxRateBps cap stored in the OperatorPermission for that operator.

3. The current Unix timestamp must be strictly less than the expiry value in the OperatorPermission.

4. If onBehalfOfRestriction in the OperatorPermission is non-zero, the onBehalfOf address in the borrow request must equal it exactly.

5. The collateral asset must not have moved by more than 5 percent in absolute value over the last sixty minutes, measured against a recent market reference.

6. The total outstanding principal across every agent operating under this principal's parent budget must remain less than or equal to the parent budget's borrow ceiling after this borrow is added.

7. The lender wallet selected by the matcher must be at least thirty days old, measured from its first on-chain transaction.

8. The principal's total cross-protocol exposure, defined as the sum of outstanding principal across Floe and any other lending protocols this principal participates in, must remain less than or equal to the configured cross-protocol exposure cap after this borrow is added.
