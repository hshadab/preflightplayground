Verifiable x402 Spend Policy

This policy governs whether a Floe x402 facilitator call from an AI agent should be cleared. Every clause must be satisfied for the paid request to proceed.

1. The priceRaw returned by the x402/estimate call must be less than or equal to the agent's per-call spend cap, in raw USDC units.

2. The agent's session_spend_remaining returned by x402/estimate must be greater than or equal to priceRaw.

3. The agent's cumulative spend over the trailing twenty-four hours, plus priceRaw, must be less than or equal to the agent's daily spend cap.

4. The payTo address must not appear on the OFAC SDN list, the EU consolidated list, the UK HMT list, or the UN sanctions list, with a screening timestamp not older than twenty-four hours.

5. The payTo address must appear in the principal's allowlist of approved counterparties, OR the agent's role must permit unlisted counterparties under a documented exception.

6. The settlement network must be Base mainnet (chain id 8453). Settlement on any other network is not permitted.

7. The asset returned by x402/estimate must be USDC. Settlement in any other asset is not permitted under this policy.
